Want to make your R analyses reproducible across computers, operating systems, and over time? Join this GhentCORR webinar to learn how {rix}, a user-friendly interface to Nix, helps you create portable and reproducible computational environments.

Featuring [Felipe Fontana Vieira](https://www.linkedin.com/in/felipe-fontana-vieira-b00258200/) (UGent), {rix} user and co-author of a tutorial on the package, this session will introduce Nix, compare it with tools such as {renv} and Docker, and demonstrate how R users can leverage its power without needing prior Nix experience.

Target audience: R users interested in improving the reproducibility, portability, and longevity of their workflows. No prior experience with Nix is required.
